# 09 — Report Card Layout (Proposal)

**Status: PROPOSED. Q-E6 is OPEN.** It stays open until the project owner personally reviews and approves all nine design choices in §6 (Q-E6.1 to Q-E6.9). Nothing in this layout is confirmed until then. The content rules it applies (four-part name, marks, percentages, grades, PASS/FAIL, outcomes, ABSENT, ranking, rounding) come from confirmed decisions C-01 to C-14 and are not reopened here.

## 1. Design principles
- **One page, A4 portrait** (210 × 297 mm), margins 12 mm. It prints cleanly in black and white: status is never shown by colour alone.
- **Bilingual labels**: Somali first, English second (e.g. "Natiijada / Result"). The Somali wording below is a **draft for native-speaker review** (Q-G6). The term labels stay "Mid Term" and "Final Term" (Q-AY1).
- **Two card types**: the **Mid Term report card** and the **Final Term report card**. There is no annual card, annual column, combined percentage or annual rank (C-02).
- **Only published results** appear (FR-RPC-05). Every figure comes from the term's result snapshot, so the card always matches the system.
- **Percentages are shown to 2 decimals, truncated, never rounded up.** Grades and PASS/FAIL are decided on exact values (C-04).

### 1.1 Mandatory content (not affected by the nine choices in §6)
Whatever is decided in §6, every report card keeps:
- **Separate Mid Term and Final Term results.** Each term's marks, percentage, grade and PASS/FAIL are calculated and shown on their own; they are never combined into an annual figure (C-02, C-14).
- The student's **four-part name**: First Name, Father's Name, Grandfather's Name, Fourth Name (C-12).
- The **overall term percentage** (2 decimals, truncated; C-04, C-05) and the **overall grade** (C-03).
- **PASS/FAIL** for the term (C-05, C-14). On the Mid Term card it is labelled as an academic progress assessment that does not affect promotion.
- On the **Final Term** card, the applicable **end-of-year status: Promoted to {next grade}, Repeats {grade}, or Graduated** (C-06, C-09).

## 3. Mid Term report card: differences from the Final Term card
- Title: "FINAL TERM" is replaced by **"MID TERM"**.
- One results block only (Mid Term marks, %, grade). There is no reference column.
- Rank: the Mid Term section and grade ranks.
- Result line: **"NATIIJADA / RESULT: PASS"** (or FAIL) with the note **"Academic progress assessment – does not affect promotion"** (C-14). There is **no** end-of-year outcome line.
- Attendance: Mid Term attendance counts.
- Grading legend, remarks, signatures and footer as on the Final Term card.

## 4. Field specification

| Block | Field | Source / rule |
|---|---|---|
| Header | Logo, school name (Somali and English), address, phone, email, registration number, motto | School profile (FR-SCH-03) |
| Header | Student photo (only if Q-E6.2 = yes) | Student record. Placeholder box if there is no photo. |
| Title | Card type, term label, academic year | Term (C-01) |
| Student | Full four-part name (First, Father's, Grandfather's, Fourth Name), admission number, grade, section, roll number, sex | Student and enrolment (C-12) |
| Results | Subject, marks /100 (2 decimals), percentage, letter grade | `result_snapshot.subject_breakdown` (C-03, C-08) |
| Results | ABSENT subject | Printed as "ABSENT – 0/100", grade F (C-07) |
| Results | EXEMPT subject | Printed as "EXEMPT", excluded from totals (GP-13) |
| Summary | Total obtained / total possible, overall %, overall grade | Snapshot totals (C-05). % truncated to 2 decimals |
| Summary | Rank in section and rank in grade ("4 of 32") | Competition ranking 1, 2, 2, 4 (C-11). Omitted for unranked students |
| Result | PASS / FAIL on both cards. Mid Term labelled "academic progress assessment – does not affect promotion" | `result_status` (C-05, C-14) |
| Outcome (Final Term only) | "Promoted to {next grade}", "Repeats {grade}", "Graduated" | `enrollment.outcome` (C-06, C-09) |
| Attendance | School days, present, late, absent, excused for the term | Attendance records within the term (FR-SAT-06). An attendance-percentage line can be added once its formula is agreed. |
| Remarks | Class teacher's remark, principal's remark | `report_card_remark` (FR-RPC-04) |
| Legend | Official grading scale, PASS rule, ABSENT rule | Grading scale version used by the snapshot (GS-6) |
| Guardian (only if Q-E6.9 = yes) | Primary guardian name and phone (C-13) | Student record |
| Signatures | Class teacher, principal, parent/guardian; school stamp area | Optional scanned signature and stamp images from school settings |
| Footer | Issue date, document number, page, optional QR verification code | FR-PDF-07 (could-have) |

## 5. Special cases
| Case | What the card shows |
|---|---|
| Student in the configured final grade who passes | Outcome "Graduated" (C-09) |
| Student who fails the Final Term | Result FAIL, outcome "Repeats {grade}" |
| Late joiner without a Final Term result (C-10) | **No Final Term report card is issued.** A one-page "Placement notice" may be printed after the School Admin's placement decision (part of the overall layout approval, Q-E6.1). |
| Result corrected after publication | The re-published version is printed. The footer shows "Revised – version N" and the new issue date. |

## 6. Design questions for the project owner (Q-E6)

**Q-E6 remains OPEN.** These are the nine outstanding report card design questions. Each has a recommended option, but **none is confirmed until the project owner personally reviews and approves all nine**. The mandatory content in §1.1 applies whatever the answers.

| ID | Question | Recommended option | Why this is recommended |
|----|----------|--------------------|-------------------------|
| Q-E6.1 | Approve the overall layout and section order (header → title → student → results → summary → result/outcome → attendance → remarks → legend → signatures → footer), including the optional late-joiner "Placement notice" (§5)? | **Approve as proposed.** | It follows the usual order of a school report (who, what, result, comments, signatures), fits on one A4 page, and already contains every mandatory item in §1.1. |
| Q-E6.2 | Include the **student photo**? | **No** for v1 (can be enabled later as a school setting). | Photos are not yet collected for every student, so many cards would show an empty box. Leaving it out also keeps personal data of minors on printed documents to a minimum and keeps bulk PDF generation lighter. |
| Q-E6.3 | Show the **Mid Term reference column** on the Final Term card, or only the Final Term? | **Show the Mid Term column**, clearly labelled "reference only – not combined". | Parents see both term results of the year on the final document, which best meets the requirement that Mid Term and Final Term results are kept and shown separately. The label and the absence of any combined figure prevent it being read as an annual result (C-02). |
| Q-E6.4 | Show both **section rank and grade rank**, or only section rank? | **Both**, as "Section: 4 of 32 · Grade: 11 of 95". | Both ranks are already computed (FR-RNK-01, C-11) at no extra cost, take one line, and the grade rank is fairer when sections differ in size or strength. |
| Q-E6.5 | Include a **parent/guardian signature** line? | **Yes.** | A signed card that returns to school confirms the family received the results. It costs one line and needs no data. |
| Q-E6.6 | Include the **QR verification code** now or later? | **Later**; print the **document number** now and reserve the footer space for the QR. | QR verification (FR-PDF-07) is a could-have that needs a public verification page and its security review. The document number already allows a manual check, and adding the QR later needs no layout change. |
| Q-E6.7 | Language: **bilingual** (Somali + English), Somali only, or English only? | **Bilingual**, Somali first. | All PDFs must support both languages (G4); one bilingual card serves every family and any receiving school, without printing two versions. |
| Q-E6.8 | Confirm the **Somali label wording** in the wireframe, or provide preferred wording? | **Accept the draft provisionally**, to be corrected by the school's native-speaker reviewer before the pilot (Q-G6). | It lets the layout be approved now without waiting for translation review. Labels are text resources, so corrections do not change the design. |
| Q-E6.9 | Print the **parent/guardian name and phone** (mandatory data, C-13) in the student block? | **No.** | The card is handed to the family, who already know these details. Staff can find the contact through the guardian contact list (FR-RPT-03). Leaving it off reduces personal data on a document that may be passed to other people. |

Once all nine are approved, Q-E6 is marked confirmed in [08 — Open Questions](08-open-questions.md) and this document's status changes from PROPOSED to CONFIRMED.
